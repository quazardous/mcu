## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-04

The first public version: the box (scheduler with backfill, priorities and
dependencies; per-project quotas, storage and pools; rootless containers with
declared resources; timing and power profiles; eGPU handling), its dashboard,
the client (`mcuctl`, `remote.sh`, an MCP server) and the docs.
